Auto-fallback: even with SPLIT_SMEM on, the global-atomic kernel is used when the required shared memory (num_children × (1 + num_classes) × 4 B) exceeds the device per-block cap (wide trees / many classes). A/B harness: SPLITUPDATE_AB=1 times GLOBAL vs SMEM head-to-head.

All in oblique_apply_projection.cu except the NODE_ID_DRSW split (node_id_drsw.cuh). Older SplitExamples kernels (ComputeSplitFlagsKernel, ScatterSplitKernel, …AllDepthKernel, CompactSplitResultsKernel) live only in *_bak1/_bak2.cu and are not compiled.
